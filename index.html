Help & support

Getting started

1. Create an account.
Use your email address to register or sign in.

2. Create a project.
Give your idea a title and describe what you want to achieve.

3. Keep it updated.
Edit the project, change its status, or remove it when appropriate.

4. Use the planner.

Connection troubleshooting

If projects fail to load, check your internet connection and confirm that the Supabase project is active and its database policies are configured.

If account confirmation is enabled, check your email for the confirmation message after registration.
