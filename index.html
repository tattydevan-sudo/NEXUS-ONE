Help & Support

Creating an account: Open Account / Sign in, enter your email and password, then create an account or sign in.

Saving projects: Sign in, open Idea Planner, describe your idea, then save it.

AI functionality: The planner sends your idea to the NEXUS ONE backend at /api/ai. The Cloudflare AI binding and backend function must be deployed.

If something fails: Check your internet connection and read the on-screen message. Never share your password or private server keys.
